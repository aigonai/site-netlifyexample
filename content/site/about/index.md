This website is one of four examples built for an article on making small tools as web pages instead of apps.

It is styled with the Topaze brand kit: Montserrat for text, IBM Plex Mono for figures, the topaz blues of the gem, and one warm imperial accent per view. Topaze is a product of [Aigon](https://aigon.ai).

The source is public at [aigonai/site-netlifyexample](https://github.com/aigonai/site-netlifyexample).
